Jubail Island, Abu Dhabi

Project S

A Waterfront Residence Defined by Privacy.

Overview

Lenval Properties is developing an exceptional beachfront mansion within an exclusive cluster of six large plots in Jubail Island’s Marfaa District. Set on an expansive 5,000-square-metre plot, the residence will offer direct private beach access, pristine waters, spectacular sunset views, and vistas of the Abu Dhabi skyline—creating a truly rare waterfront living experience in Abu Dhabi.

Jubail Island’s mangroves, pristine waters, and rich biodiversity offer a rare sense of privacy and connection to nature, minutes from Abu Dhabi.

Seamless Beachfront Living

For Project S, we reimagined the traditional layout by placing the master suite on the ground floor, with direct access to the beach. A private resort-style plunge pool and secluded outdoor shower and bathroom flow seamlessly from the indoor bathroom, creating an intimate waterfront retreat.

Elevated Entertaining

We dedicated the second floor to formal entertaining, taking full advantage of its panoramic skyline views. A discreet, carefully planned access route allows guests to enter without passing through the family’s private living areas.

Outdoor Living

The outdoor pool deck connects seamlessly with a series of water features that flow throughout the estate, creating a continuous dialogue with water and enhancing the property’s sense of calm and tranquillity.

Fitness & Zen Garden

We designed the gym with uninterrupted beach views to one side and direct access to an intimate Zen garden on the other. This tranquil outdoor space offers a private setting for relaxation, yoga and meditation, while also serving as an open-air fitness area.

Abu Dhabi Royal Equestrian Arts (ADREA)

Located on Jubail Island, this world-class academy celebrates classical horsemanship through exceptional training, performance and equestrian culture.

An Exclusive Marfaa Enclave

A rare cluster of just six expansive beachfront plots in Jubail Island’s Marfaa District, offering exceptional privacy, pristine waters and uninterrupted views of the Abu Dhabi skyline.

Bada Al Jubail

An exclusive waterfront community of individually crafted mansions, shaped by internationally acclaimed practices to create a refined dialogue between architecture and nature.

Gordonstoun School

Set to make its Gulf-region debut on Jubail Island, Gordonstoun will offer a world-renowned British education centred on character, independence and leadership.

Marfaa Al Jubail Marina

A refined waterfront destination centred around a 66-berth marina, offering residents effortless access to boating, leisure and an elegant island lifestyle.
